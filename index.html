Thoughtful care for little ones

Little Hands,
Special Care

Little moments deserve thoughtful care. Explore delicate designs for special family occasions, with attention to ingredient transparency and individual skin sensitivity.

  • Parents or guardians must be involved in booking and give consent.
  • We will clearly disclose the mehndi ingredients before application.
  • We do not use black mehndi or products containing PPD (para-phenylenediamine).
  • We cannot promise that mehndi is irritation-free or suitable for every baby.

For a one-year-old: please check with your pediatrician before applying mehndi, especially if your child has sensitive skin or a history of skin reactions.

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Everything you need to know before booking your mehndi experience with theZebaCone.

Do you offer designs for baby showers?

Yes, you can enquire about personalized designs for baby showers and Godh Bharai celebrations. Contact us to discuss your preferred style and appointment availability.

Do you provide designs for ring ceremonies?

Yes, you can enquire about elegant designs for ring ceremonies, engagements, and pre-wedding celebrations. We recommend booking early to secure your preferred date.

Do you offer designs for children?

We offer simple, playful design options for children. Ingredient transparency and individual skin sensitivity are important, particularly for very young children. We do not use black mehndi or products containing PPD. A parent or guardian must be involved in booking and consent. Please discuss suitability with us before booking; for a one-year-old, consult a pediatrician before application.

Do you offer designs for elders?

Yes, you can enquire about simple, elegant, and traditional designs for mothers, grandmothers, and other family members.

How early should I book?

We recommend contacting us as early as possible, particularly for weddings, baby showers, ring ceremonies, and group bookings. Availability depends on the date and event requirements.

What ingredients are in your mehndi?

We prepare mehndi at home and can share the current ingredient list before an appointment. We only describe ingredients as naturally sourced when verified. Skin reactions are possible, so please tell us about any sensitivities or previous reactions before booking.

Do you provide home service?

Absolutely. We offer home service in Bhilwara and other locations by prior arrangement, including destination weddings across India.

Do you take destination wedding bookings within India?

Yes! We travel across India for destination weddings. Travel and accommodation are arranged based on your event location.
